03 · free support

No budget? No problem

There are plenty of non-monetary ways to help keep my projects growing.

  1. 01

    Share my projects

    Tell a friend, post on social media, or link to CanonTrails, BookList4U, BestScreenTester, or Animaruni from a forum. Word of mouth is the best support.

  2. 02

    Use buy links

    When you purchase games or books through affiliate links on CanonTrails or BookList4U, I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

  3. 03

    Report errors & bugs

    Spot something wrong or broken? Let me know on GitHub or via email. Accurate data is the backbone of these archives.

  4. 04

    Suggest improvements

    Ideas for features, UI refinements, or new project concepts are always welcome. Every suggestion helps shape the roadmap.

Thank you.

Whether you donate, share, or just use my projects to find your next adventure — you're helping keep this work alive. I appreciate every one of you.
